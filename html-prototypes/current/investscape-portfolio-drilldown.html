Macro to micro: from the portfolio table to the deal underneath it.

Two connected screens. The macro view is the portfolio table from the ecosystem concept — every row is now clickable. Clicking a property populates the micro view below: a full deal statement laid out exactly like the Mortgage & Rent Analysis Template v2 spreadsheet — Inputs, Purchase Info waterfall, Mortgage terms, and the Income / Expenses / Net Performance statement with Monthly and Annual columns.

All figures in the micro view are computed live in the page from each property's inputs, so every drill-down stays internally consistent — Canadian semi-annual mortgage compounding for CA properties, monthly for US, per the dual-market formula engine. The first row — Unit 1804, Burnaby — carries the $550,000 Template v2 base case (payment within ~$1 of the sheet, whose stored rate rounds to 4.54%). US properties demonstrate the market toggle: HOA Fee label, no BC Property Transfer Tax.
