Lighthouse Research Ltd. — Development Studio Drill-Down v1.1

Development Studio: from napkin feasibility to the lender package.

Twelve screens built directly on Document 07 (the field map dissected from the five developer proformas) — every workspace tab now has its own frame. The systems: the dual-path New Project fork (Quick Proforma or Full Model — quick expands into full at any time), three project types, the stage pipeline and photo thumbnails with an upload box on every project card, an eye toggle that hides dead deals from the hub, the Under Consideration pipeline, snapshot scenarios with a variance column, budget vs. actuals, the average-outstanding-factor interest mechanic, the Sources ≡ Uses balance badge, the Partner Split Calculator, the parcel-assembly and PTT bracket engine, the unit price list with roll-ups, the breakeven ladder and sensitivity heatmap, the phase Gantt, and the AI-readable file repository.

Interactive: the Quick Proforma is computed live from the Gilley model (defaults land within 0.1% of its headline: $43.75M cost, $12.6M profit, ~28.9% ROC) — edit any input. Toggle KPI normalization on Screen C, switch Actuals on Screen D, flip the waterfall variant on Screen F, hide a project with any eye icon then reveal with “Show hidden” on Screen A, drag the gold AI bubble anywhere. 796 Main figures come from the Document 07 validation set. Buddy note: capture as separate Figma frames — the New Project modal (open), Actuals on and off, both waterfall variants, each normalization mode, and the hub with hidden deals revealed.
