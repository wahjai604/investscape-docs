Lighthouse Research Ltd. — v2 Unified Shell, Addendum A

The rooms nobody's stood in yet: signing up, choosing a plan, day one, and the wall.

Doc 09's audit found four structural gaps shared by every mockup so far: no empty state, no signup or plan-selection screen, Free tier never actually rendered, and the liability disclaimer missing from every metrics surface. This addendum fills each one — plus a fifth screen for the downgrade rule (Pro→Free makes excess deals read-only) since it was already the natural next stop. Every frame below reuses the unified shell's exact tokens, component classes, and chrome-generating functions verbatim; nothing in the visual system was reinvented, only assembled from what already existed. Screens One and Two are intentionally chrome-free, matching the Login screen's own precedent — nobody sees the app ribbon before they have an account.

Interactive: fill the signup card (nothing persists, it's a mockup), pick a role, pick a plan, and — on Screen Four — click the locked Development Studio tab to see the wall a Free or Pro user actually hits. That click has never done anything anywhere in the system until this file.
