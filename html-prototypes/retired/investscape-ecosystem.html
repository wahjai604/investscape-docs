Lighthouse Research Ltd. — Ecosystem Concept

InvestScape, as a working system.

Four screens sharing one navigation shell, one type system, and one set of components — the way an actual product would, rather than four disconnected hero shots. Each screen borrows a functional pattern from a category leader (portfolio tracking, research feeds, workspace notes, live charting) without copying any of their layouts, colors, or content verbatim.

Everything below is original layout and placeholder data built to explore feel and structure — not a reproduction of any existing product's design or branding.
