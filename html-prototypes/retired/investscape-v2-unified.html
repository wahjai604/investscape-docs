Lighthouse Research Ltd. — v2 Unified Shell

One shell, eight rooms: the full InvestScape platform in a single frame system.

Every screen now shares the same generated global chrome — the two-tier ribbon with collapsible search, Customize mode with lock, the language globe, notifications, and the user profile menu with sign-out — plus the draggable, collapsible AI bubble on every page. Research and Workspace return from ecosystem v1, Market Intel joins from the analyzer suite, and Development Studio debuts behind the Enterprise lock, built from the five developer proformas. A visualization layer runs throughout: allocation donut and equity bars in Portfolio, an income waterfall and amortization curve in the Deal Analyzer, and cost donut, draw S-curve, sensitivity heatmap, and breakeven ladder in Development Studio.

Interactive: expand the search, switch languages (nav + labels translate on every frame), open the profile menu and sign out (demo toast), toggle Customize and drag KPI cards then lock, filter and hide portfolio rows, open export menus, drag any frame's AI bubble. Development Studio figures are computed live from one in-page model so the budget, KPIs, and heatmap always agree. Buddy note: capture key states (search open, customize on, AI panel open, profile menu open) as separate Figma frames.

Capitalization Rate — IRV
F-404
R = I ÷ V · V = I ÷ R · I = V × R
One relationship, three uses: measure yield, price from income, or infer income from price. InvestScape shows market cap and yield-on-cost separately — never conflated.
Core engineAll tiers
Debt Service Coverage Ratio
F-502
DSCR = NOI ÷ Annual Debt Service
How many times over the income covers the mortgage — 1.25 is the common commercial floor; below 1.0 the building can't carry its loan.
CommercialLoan Sizing
Leverage State
F-505
cost of debt < unlevered yield → positive
Cheap debt concentrates a building's spread onto smaller equity and raises the equity return; expensive debt runs the same math in reverse. Named on every financed deal.

Net Present Value
F-410
NPV = Σ [CFₜ ÷ (1+r)ᵗ] − Investment
At your required return, is the deal worth more than it costs — and by how many dollars? The better ranking tool across deal sizes.
