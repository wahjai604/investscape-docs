Cc: legislativeservices@burnaby.ca
Subject: InvestScape — official Zoning Bylaw 2026 records and historical zoning data

I am developing InvestScape, a commercial real estate analysis software product. I would appreciate assistance identifying the official records for Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, and the historical zoning evidence available for property analysis.

Your zoning rewrite page reports adoption on June 9, 2026 and an effective date of July 1, 2026. Could you please provide or link to the complete enacted by-law, including Parts 1–7 and its incorporated schedules, the commencement clause or instrument, and any transitional or savings provisions?
