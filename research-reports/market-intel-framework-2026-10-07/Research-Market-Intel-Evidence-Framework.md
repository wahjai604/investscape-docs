# Research to Market Intel evidence framework

Status: accepted planning baseline, not implemented. See [index](README.md).

Research owns the qualitative catalog, rights, editorial review and publication lifecycle. Market Intel consumes approved geographically associated evidence. Keep Community independent; it is not an editorial evidence source by default.

## Research experience
Search/list/detail approved published items by topic, geography, source family and dates. Six source families: government, journalism, commercial research, academic/applied research, associations and Lighthouse. Lighthouse primary/secondary analysis retains methodology and underlying-source rights.
Search cards disclose title, attribution, source link, topic, geographic scope, publication/review dates and freshness. Detail shows permitted summary or original-source link; unknown rights never expose full text. Link-only, summary-permitted and withheld are distinct.
Empty, pagination, unavailable detail, broken source link and API error have separate states. Direct detail applies the same publication/audience checks as search.

## Minimum catalog fields
Stable item ID/revision; canonical URL/title/publisher/authors/source family; topic tags; canonical geography IDs and scope/matching basis; publication date and precision or unknown; retrieved/review dates; editorial owner; publication state/audience; rights state/evidence/reviewer/review date; permitted summary; corrections/withdrawal reason; internal audit references.
Withdrawn summaries disappear from member list/detail and downstream AI/cache reads; audit record remains internally. Publication/access changes version downstream evidence and trigger invalidation. Previously exported images cannot be remotely erased; sharing design must disclose this limitation.

## Connections
Research -> Market Intel opens geographic context with evidence reference.
Market Intel -> Research opens permitted detail.
AI uses the same approved permission-filtered evidence, citations and freshness; no hidden content exposed through summaries/embeddings.
An article may cover multiple cities or a province/state. A site pin requires a grounded location; inference must be disclosed. Research may explain metrics but does not establish causality or investment suitability by itself.

Initial scope: Vancouver/Toronto city and metro, BC/Ontario context, Arizona/Texas statewide and Canada/US national context. US local markets unselected.
Open: approved catalog, owner/review process, audience rules, item-by-item feeds/cadence/rights. Public availability is not reuse permission. No ingestion or module schema authorized.
